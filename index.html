Exercise 1 • Posterior Chain

Bench Hip Thrust (Glute Bridge)

Form-Checked Guide

Step-by-Step Directions:

  1. Sit on your yoga mat with your upper back and shoulder blades resting firmly against the long edge of your workout bench.
  2. Bend your knees with feet planted flat on the floor, hip-width apart.
  3. Push firmly through your heels (not your toes) and drive your hips upward until your torso and thighs form a flat horizontal tabletop.
  4. Squeeze your glutes tight at the top for 2 full seconds.
  5. Gently lower your hips down until you almost touch the mat, then immediately repeat.
