Low-Impact Corrective Strength & Nutrition

4-Day Corrective Strength Routine

Schedule: 30-min morning brisk walk + 20-min corrective session (4 days/week).

3-Minute Joint Warm-Up (Perform Before Every Session):

1. Supine Belly Breathing (1 min): Lie on back, breathe deeply into abdomen to relax back muscles.

2. Bodyweight Glute Bridges (1 min): 10 gentle reps to activate hips without weight.

3. Cat-Cow on Floor (1 min): Slow spinal flexion and extension to lubricate vertebrae.

Exercise 1: Bench Hip Thrust (Glute Bridge) — Form Check

Joint-Safety Priority

Step-by-Step Directions:

  1. Sit on mat with upper back/shoulder blades against bench edge.
  2. Feet flat, hip-width apart, knees bent.
  3. Drive upward firmly through your heels until torso and thighs form a flat tabletop.
  4. Squeeze glutes tight at the top for 2 full seconds.
  5. Lower hips slowly until almost touching mat, then repeat.

Arm & Neck Safety Checkpoints:

  • ✓ Upper arms on bench: Rest triceps/elbows flat on top of the bench pad to support torso.
  • ✓ Alternative: Cross arms loosely over chest.
  • ✕ Do not place hands on floor: Keeping hands on the floor twists the wrists and pinches neck/trapezius muscles.
  • ✓ Gaze: Look forward toward your knees, not straight up at ceiling.

Prescription: 3 sets of 10–12 reps. Rest 60 seconds between sets.

Exercise 2: Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row

3 sets × 10 reps | Starting Weight: 4–6 kg per hand

Lie chest-down on an incline bench. Pull dumbbells upward toward hips, keeping elbows close to your sides. Squeeze back muscles without straining the neck.

Joint Safeguard: Bench supports torso weight, eliminating lower-back strain entirely.

Exercise 3: Dead Bug (Core Stability)

3 sets × 8 reps per side | Bodyweight

Lie flat on back with arms pointing to ceiling and knees at 90°. Slowly extend opposite arm and leg while pressing lower back flat into the mat.

Joint Safeguard: Zero lumbar twisting or crunching. Trains deep abdominal stabilizers.

Day 2: Upper Body Stability & Rotator Cuff

1. Floor Dumbbell Press

3 sets × 10 reps | 5–8 kg

Lie flat on mat. Press dumbbells upward. Lower elbows until triceps tap the floor, then press up.

Safeguard: Floor stops shoulder hyperextension.

2. Band Pull-Apart

3 sets × 15 reps | Light Band

Hold band straight ahead at chest level. Pull band apart until it touches chest, squeezing shoulder blades.

Safeguard: Relieves neck tightness by activating upper back.

3. Suitcase Carry / March

3 sets × 45 sec per side | 6–8 kg

Hold one dumbbell at side like a suitcase. Walk tall with shoulders level, bracing core.

Safeguard: Trains core anti-lateral flexion without spine strain.

Day 3: Knee Alignment & Posterior Chain

1. Low Box Step-Ups

3 sets × 8 reps per leg | Bodyweight

Step onto a low bench or step. Drive through whole foot to stand tall. Lower down under control.

Safeguard: Controlled single-leg knee tracking; no popping.

2. Band Hamstring Curls

3 sets × 12 reps | Light Band

Loop band around ankle and bench leg. Lie on stomach or stand, curling heel toward glute.

Safeguard: Strengthens tendon behind knee directly.

3. Bird-Dog

3 sets × 8 reps per side | Bodyweight

On all fours, extend right arm and left leg straight out. Hold 2 seconds, switch sides.

Safeguard: True spinal stability without rotational stress.

Day 4: Postural Strength & Core Integration

1. Neutral-Grip Dumbbell Rows

3 sets × 10 reps | 5–7 kg

Bench-supported row with palms facing inward. Keeps shoulders completely free of impingement.

Safeguard: Protects rotator cuff tendons.

2. Isometric Glute Bridge Hold

3 sets × 25–30 sec hold | Bodyweight

Drive hips upward and hold at top position. Keeps continuous tension on hip extensors.

Safeguard: Joint-friendly tendon conditioning.

3. Band External Rotations

3 sets × 12 reps per side | Light Band

Tuck a towel roll under elbow. Rotate forearm outward away from abdomen.

Safeguard: Fixes rounded shoulder posture and neck strain.

Plain-English Exercise Dictionary

• Sets & Reps: 3 sets of 10 reps means you perform the movement 10 times, rest 1 minute, and repeat that sequence 3 times total.

• Heel Drive: Pushing exclusively through your heels prevents knee shear and protects against knee popping sounds.

• Neutral Spine: Keeping your natural back curve straight without tucking or hyperextending.
