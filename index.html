Plain-English Exercise Dictionary

Sets and Reps: "3 sets × 10 reps" means doing 10 repetitions, resting for the listed time, and repeating that cycle 3 times in total.
Tempo (2-2-2): 2 seconds lifting, 2 seconds holding, 2 seconds lowering. Slow is safer for joints.
Heel Drive: Pressing through your heels rather than the balls of your feet. This works the glutes and takes load off the knees.
Neutral Spine: Keeping the spine in its natural S-curve without bending forward or arching backward.
Isometric: Holding a position still while the muscle works, for example a bridge hold.
Anti-rotation / Anti-extension: Core exercises where the job is to stop your trunk from twisting or arching.

Optional: back up the log to Google Sheets

Browsers cannot write to your Google account without a sign-in step, so this uses a tiny script that you own.

  1. Create a new Google Sheet, then Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Paste the script below, then Deploy → New deployment → Web app, execute as Me, access Anyone.
  3. Paste the web-app URL here and press Save URL, then Send history.
function doPost(e) {
  var rows = JSON.parse(e.postData.contents);
  var sh = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSheet();
  sh.clearContents();
  sh.appendRow(["Date","Weight","Knee","Back","Shoulder","Walk","Routine","Steps","Apple kcal","Total kcal"]);
  rows.forEach(function (r) { sh.appendRow(r); });
  return ContentService.createTextOutput("ok");
}
